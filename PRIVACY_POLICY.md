# Privacy Policy for ProfiFlow

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Introduction

ProfiFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information:** Email address, name, and authentication credentials when you create an account
- **Resume Data:** Personal information, work experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, and languages you enter into the app
- **Profile Information:** Any additional information you provide in your user profile

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, and app version
- **Usage Data:** App features used, session duration, and interaction patterns
- **Local Storage:** App preferences, theme settings, and language preferences stored on your device

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the resume building service
- Generate PDF resumes based on your input
- Authenticate and secure your account
- Save your resume data across devices
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Provide customer support

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage:** Resume data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms
- **Cloud Storage:** If you sign in with Google or email, your data is stored securely using Firebase services
- **Encryption:** All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- **Authentication:** We use Firebase Authentication to secure your account

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase:** For authentication and cloud storage
- **Google Sign-In:** For optional Google account authentication

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:
- When required by law
- To protect our rights and safety
- With your explicit consent

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your resume data
- Opt-out of data collection (by not using the app)

## Data Retention

- Resume data is retained as long as your account is active
- You can delete your data at any time through the app settings
- Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed

## Children's Privacy

ProfiFlow is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
